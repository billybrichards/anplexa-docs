## 🎨 Content Quality Standards

### E-E-A-T Signals
- **Experience**: Author bios showing 30-day hands-on testing of each platform
- **Expertise**: Technical explanations of memory systems, LLMs, TTS technology
- **Authoritativeness**: Cite research (MIT Technology Review, APA), industry reports, statistics
- **Trustworthiness**: Transparent pros/cons, credit competitors fairly, privacy warnings

### Writing Guidelines
- **Tone**: Professional, helpful, non-judgmental about AI companion use
- **Length**: 2,000-5,000 words depending on keyword difficulty
- **Readability**: 8th-grade reading level, short paragraphs, bullet points
- **Visuals**: Image every 200-300 words to break up text
- **Data-Driven**: Cite statistics, link to sources, use current 2026 data
- **Balanced**: Credit competitors fairly, don't oversell Anplexa

### Universal Article Structure
1. **Introduction** (200-400 words): Hook, context, article preview
2. **Quick Answer Box** (100-150 words): Immediate value for scanners
3. **Main Content** (2,000-4,500 words): H2/H3 hierarchy, rich media
4. **FAQ Section** (300-500 words): Schema markup for featured snippets
5. **Conclusion** (150-250 words): Recap, final recommendation, strong CTA

### Anplexa CTAs (3 per article)
- **Early CTA** (after intro): "Take our 2-minute personality quiz"
- **Mid CTA** (after main content): "Discover Anplexa's 6-persona system for business"
- **Final CTA** (conclusion): "Try ChatPMU for your beauty business - first 6 messages free"

### Strategic Positioning

1. **Awareness**: Users discover Anplexa while researching consumer apps
2. **Differentiation**: Content clearly distinguishes B2B/vertical SaaS from consumer romantic companions
3. **Authority**: Comprehensive market knowledge builds trust and credibility
4. **Conversion**: CTAs emphasize ChatPMU business use case, not personal companion

### Competitive Moat
Anplexa's unique advantages are emphasized throughout content:
- **6-Persona System**: vs one-size-fits-all consumer apps
- **Vertical Customization**: ChatPMU (PMU industry) vs generic companions
- **Enterprise Security**: HIPAA-ready, JWT, Helmet.js vs consumer-focused
- **Self-Hosted**: Ollama on-premise vs third-party API dependencies
- **White-Label**: Platform for entrepreneurs vs standalone product
- **Pricing Power**: $50-300/month B2B justified by ROI vs $3-16 consumer

ChatPMU demonstrates repeatable model:
1. Identify niche industry ($2B+ TAM)
2. Build industry-specific features

4. Charge 5-20x consumer pricing (justified by business ROI)
5. Expand to adjacent verticals (beauty → healthcare → e-commerce)
