# CONTENT QUALITY STANDARDS

## E-E-A-T Signals
**Experience**: Author bios showing 30-day testing of each platform
**Expertise**: Technical explanations of memory systems, LLMs, TTS
**Authoritativeness**: Cite research (MIT, APA), industry reports, statistics
**Trustworthiness**: Transparent pros/cons, competitor credit where due, privacy warnings

## Writing Guidelines
- **Tone**: Professional, helpful, non-judgmental about AI companion use
- **Length**: 2,000-5,000 words depending on keyword difficulty
- **Readability**: 8th-grade reading level, short paragraphs, bullet points
- **Visuals**: Image every 200-300 words, break up text
- **Data**: Cite statistics, link to sources, use current 2026 data
- **Balance**: Credit competitors fairly, don't oversell Anplexa

## Quality Checklist (Before Publishing)
- [ ] Primary keyword in H1, H2, first paragraph, conclusion
- [ ] LSI keywords naturally distributed (2-3% density)
- [ ] 8-12 internal links to related content
- [ ] 3-5 external links to authoritative sources
- [ ] 10+ images with keyword-rich alt text
- [ ] FAQ section with schema markup
- [ ] 3 Anplexa CTAs strategically placed
- [ ] Meta title <60 characters, description <160 characters
- [ ] Mobile-responsive tables and images
- [ ] Readability score 60+ (Flesch Reading Ease)
- [ ] Plagiarism check passed (100% original)
- [ ] Fact-check all statistics and claims
- [ ] Proofread for grammar/spelling errors
