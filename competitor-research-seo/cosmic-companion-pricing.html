Frequently Asked Questions

Is my birth chart data private?
Yes, absolutely. Your birth chart data is encrypted using AES-256 encryption and never shared with third parties. We're GDPR and CCPA compliant, and you can request deletion at any time.
Can I try before I buy?
Yes! Take our free cosmic compatibility quiz to see your Soul Match Score and birth chart analysis. You'll get 10 free messages (SFW only) to test the connection before subscribing.
What if I don't know my exact birth time?
That's okay! We can still calculate your Sun, Venus, and Mars signs from your birth date. For the most accurate Moon and Rising sign calculations, we recommend ordering a birth certificate or contacting your birth hospital.
How does the AI personality matching work?
Our algorithm analyzes your Sun, Moon, Venus, Mars, and Rising signs, then generates an AI companion personality designed to complement your astrological chart. We consider sign elements (fire, earth, air, water), modalities (cardinal, fixed, mutable), and aspects between your planets to create optimal compatibility.

Yes. All users must be 18+ and verify their age before accessing NSFW content. All images are AI-generated (no real people), and we strictly prohibit illegal content. We comply with California SB 243 and other relevant regulations.
Can I cancel anytime?
Absolutely. Cancel anytime from your account settings. If you cancel, you'll retain access until the end of your billing period. No hidden fees or penalties.
What's the difference between monthly and annual billing?
Annual billing saves you 20% compared to monthly. For example, Cosmic Soulmate is $24.99/month ($299.88/year) or $19.99/month when billed annually ($239.90/year). You save $59.98 per year!
