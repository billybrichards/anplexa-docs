## 🚨 Common Pitfalls to Avoid

### Content Mistakes
- ❌ **Keyword stuffing**: Natural language, 2-3% density max
- ❌ **Thin content**: Minimum 2,000 words for competitive keywords
- ❌ **Poor structure**: Use H2/H3 hierarchy, not flat H2s
- ❌ **No visuals**: Add image every 200-300 words
- ❌ **Ignoring mobile**: 60%+ traffic is mobile

### Strategic Mistakes
- ❌ **Competing directly**: Don't position Anplexa as "better Nomi"
- ❌ **Pure consumer focus**: Always include B2B angle
- ❌ **Weak CTAs**: Make ChatPMU value clear (ROI, not just features)
- ❌ **No internal linking**: Link articles together (8-12 per page)
- ❌ **Inconsistent publishing**: Stick to schedule

### Technical Mistakes
- ❌ **Slow page speed**: Optimize images, use CDN
- ❌ **No schema markup**: Implement Article, FAQ, Comparison schemas
- ❌ **Broken links**: Test all links before publishing
- ❌ **Missing meta tags**: Title <60 chars, description <160 chars
- ❌ **No mobile optimization**: Responsive design is essential
